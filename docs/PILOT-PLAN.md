# OfflineAI School — School Pilot Plan

## Pilot objective
Validate offline learning, personalization, synchronization and teacher learning signals.

## Initial cohort
1–2 Zimbabwean schools; Grade 6; Mathematics Fractions; Science Density; 20–50 learners.

## Success measures
- Core lessons work without continuous internet.
- Attempts persist after app restart.
- Recommendations respond to performance.
- Offline attempts queue safely.
- Successful synchronization transfers queued attempts.
- Teachers can identify topics needing support.
- Learners find explanations understandable.

## Protocol
Week 0: installation, consent/privacy briefing and teacher orientation.
Week 1: baseline diagnostic.
Weeks 2–3: guided use.
Week 4: post-assessment and teacher feedback.
After pilot: analyze learning, usability, reliability and expansion readiness.

## Safeguards
Collect minimum necessary learner data, obtain appropriate consent for minors, protect individual results and keep AI recommendations subordinate to teacher judgment.
